# Rights and provenance

Every recording published here is **public domain**, released by the rights holder. Each recording's
`metadata.json` carries the statement and its basis; the transcripts are **CC0 1.0** (see `LICENSE`).

Two rules keep that claim honest:

1. **A rights entry is per recording, never per repository.** A future deposit that is *not* cleared
   must carry `status: unknown` and must not be added until it is. "Public domain because it is old"
   is not a basis — 1971 cassette recordings of living people's voices need the holder's release.
2. **Provenance is recorded where it cannot be separated from the file.** `metadata.json` names who
   digitised it and when, and `notes.md` records the capture measurements, what was cropped, which
   engine transcribed it and which readings are still guesswork. A transcript exported without its
   notes loses the ability to say which words are uncertain.

If you are a rights holder or a participant and want something changed or removed, open an issue —
removal requests are honoured without argument.
