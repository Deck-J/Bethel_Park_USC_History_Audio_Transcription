### Left exactly as heard (not corroborated)

- **"Decman City, Washington"** (00:46:46) — George Marshall's letter about Jefferson College
  closing. Checked Deemston (a real Washington Co. borough), Donegal, Dutch Fork; nothing fits
  the phrase or the "city" wording. Possibly a suppressed oath ("that d—d city, Washington")
  or "that Democratic city" (Washington College was Jefferson's political rival), but neither
  is provable. Unresolved.
- **"225th anniversary"** (01:04:29 and 01:27:02) — correct as spoken; see Dating above.
- **"Paul Caulgolden, C-A-U-L-G-O-L-D-E-N"** (00:41:45) — the man who told Ann about Spencer
  Watson. No match found for the spelled-out surname.
- **"Hiram Holtz"**, **"James S.B."**, **"Thompson Fife"**, **"Robert Johnston"** on the 1850
  pew indenture — Fife, Johnston, Wilson and Holtz are all attested Bethel surnames
  (Thompson Fife, 1806–1860, is buried at Bethel), but the indenture itself has not been seen,
  so only the already-plausible spellings stand.
- **"Frank Ear"** (the artist), **"Mates family / Grace Mates"**, **"Thomas Cadoux"**
  (Marshall's travelling companion), **"Russell Low"** (communion token), **"Rev. Dean and
  Stella Walder"** — no corroboration either way.
- Lower-case passages (roughly 00:23:56–00:36:42 and 01:26–01:30) are Whisper casing artefacts, left untouched
  rather than silently re-capitalised.
