# Appendix A — editorial notes

## The typescript's habit of printing E as O

This machine prints a closed *e* that reads as *o* — the single largest source of noise in any machine
reading of this scan. Letters read as `o` have been restored where context, other occurrences on the
same page, and the historical record all agree:

| as printed | read as | why |
|---|---|---|
| Bothol | Bethel | Bethel Park / Bethel Church throughout |
| Fifo | Fife | Fife family; confirmed by Upper St. Clair records (Nathaniel, Laurence, William James Fife) |
| Woll | Well | context only |
| prind pal | principal | context only |
| Modowell | McDowell | the McDowell family, named in the same breath |
| Bo adling / Boadling | Beadling | a real Bethel Park locality |
| Ruthorford | Rutherford | Rutherford Acres / Rutherford shopping centre |
| Poollet | Poellot | Poellot's hardware, per local history |
| Simpson farm | Sampson farm | the farm and farm market on the road past Peters Creek Church; the page reads *Sampson* (machine OCR had "Simpson") |
| Mine #3 | Mine #3 | kept as printed |

Everything else is left exactly as typed, including "Its been years", "there ancestors", "one and and
the animals", "catacorner", "MC MILLAN", "con-tinued", "Penn-sylvania", "spelled out letter by letter"
sequences, and the typist's own mix of *Snowden* / *Snowdon*.

## Flags in the text

- **[sic: 1870's]** — the typescript has "the early 1970's" in a passage about a man born around 1870.
  Left as typed, correction flagged.
- **[illegible]** — where the scan crops the right margin (notably printed p. 33) or the ribbon is
  faint. These are missing words, not unreadable ink; the line is truncated in the original scan.
- **[handwritten: …]** — the caption on the photograph page.

The name of the interviewer's typist is **Mrs. T. R. Stump**; the interviewer is **June Ellington**,
though some renderings of the typescript read "Juno" — the closed-o habit again.

| Brush Run Road absent | present |
| Sampson farm absent | present |
| Foliart as "Follart" | Foliart (spelled out in the text: F O L I A R T) |

| 62 of 64 pages covered | every page accounted for |

## Still open

- Printed **p. 33** has a cropped right margin; roughly a dozen line-ends are lost.
- The surname **Baldesberger** appears with three spellings in the typescript (Baldesborger /
  Baldesberger); the typescript spells it out once as B A L D E S B E R G E R, which is the form used
  in the transcription.
- Some dates are the speaker's own uncertain recollection (e.g. Bob Stilley's killing, "either 1930 or
  1931") and are not errors of transcription.
