# Transcription seed — J.D. Patterson oral history

**Revision 2.** Rebuilt from the page-by-page transcription of the page images rather than the machine OCR, so the
spellings are the ones the pages actually carry: *Sampson* farm (not "Simpson"), *Foliart* (not "Follart"),
*Detling* (not "Dotling").

**Purpose.** Vocabulary and prompt seed for ASR of the Patterson recordings (`JD Patterson - Side A`, `Side B`).
A seed is a **hypothesis generator, not evidence**: it biases the decoder toward these spellings so "Fife" is not
transcribed "Fyffe" and "Irishtown" not "Irish Town". It must never be used to overwrite what the tape says.

**Confidence key.** *high* = corroborated in Upper St. Clair / Bethel Park historical sources · *document* = read from
the transcript itself · *low* = uncertain reading, check the page image.
